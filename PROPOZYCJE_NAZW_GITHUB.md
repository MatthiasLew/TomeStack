# 🚀 English Name Ideas for GitHub Project

Here are the best, catchy English names designed for a book collection & edition tracking application on GitHub.

## 🌟 Top English Names

### 1. **TomeStack** / **TomeKeeper** ⭐ *(Top Recommendation)*
* **Why:** "Tome" (a large, scholarly, or historic book/volume) sounds classy and collector-focused. Paired with "Stack" or "Keeper", it immediately suggests tracking series and volumes.
* **Tagline:** *"TomeStack – The completionist book tracker & edition catalog."*
* **Repo:** `github.com/your-username/tomestack`

### 2. **Compleat** / **SeriesComplete**
* **Why:** "Compleat" (the archaic spelling of complete) or "SeriesComplete" points straight to your core USP: helping collectors finish 100% of book series without missing volumes.
* **Tagline:** *"Never miss a volume again. Track editions and complete your book series."*
* **Repo:** `github.com/your-username/series-complete`

### 3. **ShelfRadar**
* **Why:** Focuses on detecting what's missing from your shelves and hunting for missing editions and deals across stores.
* **Tagline:** *"ShelfRadar – Find missing volumes, compare bookstore prices, and complete your shelf."*
* **Repo:** `github.com/your-username/shelfradar`

### 4. **Omnibus** / **OmniFolio**
* **Why:** In publishing, an *omnibus* is a collection of works or series. It has an intellectual, prestigious ring to it.
* **Repo:** `github.com/your-username/omnifolio`

### 5. **EditionHunt** / **EditionCraft**
* **Why:** For collectors who care about specific prints (hardcovers, 1st editions, illustrated releases, publisher variants).
* **Repo:** `github.com/your-username/editionhunt`

## 📄 Ready GitHub README.md in English

```markdown
# 📚 TomeStack – The Series Completionist & Edition Tracker

> Never lose track of a series again. Track owned volumes, uncover every publisher edition, compare real-time prices across bookstores, and browse complete author bibliographies.

## ✨ Key Features
- 📈 **Series Completion Tracker** – Real-time progress bar for book sagas (e.g. *6/8 volumes owned [75%]*).
- 🎯 **Missing Volumes Radar** – An instant checklist of what's missing from your shelf with one-click store searches.
- 🏷️ **Book Edition Explorer** – Compare publisher prints, first editions, hardcovers, paperbacks, and ISBNs.
- 💰 **Bookstore Price Comparison** – Compare prices from major stores (Empik, Świat Książki, TaniaKsiążka, Allegro, etc.).
- ✍️ **Full Author Bibliographies** – Click any author to see every series, standalone book, and anthology they have ever released.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support** – Instant toggle between Polish and English.
```
